All years at the same instant. Each dot is one year. At every frame, all years are shown simultaneously at the same calendar position (month + sub-monthly step). Watch the whole cloud of years move together through phase space as the annual cycle advances. 60 frames = 12 months × 5 sub-steps (one dot per year per frame). Color runs blue (earliest year) → red (most recent). Frames with fewer dots indicate months not yet published for recent years.
