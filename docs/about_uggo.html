About

I work at the intersection of oceanographic science and software engineering, building data pipelines, processing observational datasets, and developing tools for operational oceanography. My background spans over 20 years across research institutions, the offshore oil and gas industry, and national data centres, with applied experience in scientific programming, data management, and oceanographic instrumentation.
