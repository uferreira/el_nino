Our Team

The Earth Science Research Alliance (ESRA) is an international collaboration focused on reconstructing the low-dimensional attractor of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation from ocean observables. Our work combines physical oceanography, nonlinear dynamics, and scientific software development to make ENSO phase-space analysis accessible and reproducible.

Fabio Vieira Machado
Physical Oceanographer · Climate Variability Researcher
Research Collaborator, ESRA — Northland, New Zealand

Physical Oceanographer and Climate Variability Researcher with more than 25 years of experience spanning environmental science, oceanography, atmospheric sciences, offshore operations, and marine geophysics. Co-author of original research on the Pacific Warm Pool as an ENSO indicator and on wavelet analysis of warm pool zonal displacement. His analytical work combines Python and MATLAB with observational datasets from satellite, tide gauge, Argo, and AUV platforms.

Victor de Amorim d'Ávila
Professor · Physical Oceanographer · Astrophysicist
Faculty of Oceanography, UERJ — Observatório Nacional, Brazil

Associate Professor at the Faculty of Oceanography of UERJ and Senior Researcher at the Observatório Nacional, where he has worked since 1976. His career spans astrophysics, astrometry, physical oceanography, and nonlinear fluid dynamics. A pioneer of phase-space methods for ENSO analysis in Brazil, he supervised the original Pacific Warm Pool research underpinning this project and co-authored foundational work on mathematical models for El Niño dynamics.
