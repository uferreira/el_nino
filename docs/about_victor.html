Victor de Amorim d'Ávila

Professor Emeritus (retired) · Physical Oceanographer · Astrophysicist

Retired · Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ) and Observatório Nacional, Brazil

Victor de Amorim d'Ávila served as Professor at the Faculty of Oceanography of the Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ) and Senior Researcher at the Observatório Nacional, where he worked from 1976 until his retirement. His career spanned astrophysics, astrometry, physical oceanography, and nonlinear fluid dynamics. He was one of the pioneers of phase-space methods for ENSO analysis in Brazil, having supervised the original Pacific Warm Pool research that underpins this project, and co-authored foundational work on mathematical models for El Niño dynamics.
