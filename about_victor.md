# Victor de Amorim d'Ávila

**Professor · Physical Oceanographer · Astrophysicist**
Faculty of Oceanography, Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ), Brazil
Observatório Nacional, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Victor de Amorim d'Ávila is Associate Professor at the Faculty of Oceanography of the Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ) and Senior Researcher at the Observatório Nacional, where he has worked since 1976. His career spans astrophysics, astrometry, physical oceanography, and nonlinear fluid dynamics. He is one of the pioneers of phase-space methods for ENSO analysis in Brazil, having supervised the original Pacific Warm Pool research that underpins this project, and co-authored foundational work on mathematical models for El Niño dynamics.
